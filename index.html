01 / Chromebook Damage Reporting

Damage Report & Tracking System

Problem

Chromebook damage was reported informally — staff used paper slips or verbal reports with no consistent record. There was no way to see which school had the most incidents, what types of damage were recurring, or confirm a student had actually submitted a report.

Solution

Built a web app with Google Apps Script featuring serial-number autocomplete (searches a live spreadsheet), photo upload to Google Drive, and instant email confirmation to the submitter plus staff notification. All submissions are logged to a structured Google Sheet automatically.

Result

100% digital submissions across 7 schools. Staff now have a live dashboard showing damage trends by school and type over rolling 30-day windows — enabling data-driven repair budgeting and identifying repeat-damage patterns.

02 / Library Chromebook Checkout

Tech Checkout & Overdue Alert System

Problem

The school library had no system to track which students borrowed Chromebooks or chargers during the day. Devices were going missing or not returned, and there was no visibility into which students were repeatedly checking out devices — a potential abuse pattern.

Solution

Built a 3-step checkout/return web app with a live "currently checked out" dashboard. A daily time-based trigger scans for overdue items and high-frequency borrowers, then emails a summary to staff — including a one-click Gmail link to alert principals with pre-filled message text.

Result

Library staff now have full visibility into every device in real time. Overdue items are flagged automatically. High-frequency borrowers (3+ checkouts/week) trigger an admin email, reducing device loss and identifying students who may need a personal device assigned.
